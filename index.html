Connection instructions

Enter the site app name found in the URL while on the HCMS. It will be the the section betweenn slashes after the app.

Then add the token from client section of the HCMS that has the correct premissions for each content type you are wishing to upload.

Click "Test connection" any time to check the site name and token are correct before uploading anything — it only reads, it never changes anything on the site.

Choose a workbook. Sheets are matched to content types by name, so their order does not matter.
